1.4 Clarification regarding Three.js
------------------------------------
Note: While initially considered, three.js is not imported, packaged, nor bundled into the published JavaScript of this site. The 3D geodesic sphere in the hero section (src/components/SphereHero.astro) and the network nodes background (src/components/NetworkNodes.astro) are implemented entirely using native HTML5 Canvas 2D trigonometric mathematics without third-party 3D engine dependencies.
